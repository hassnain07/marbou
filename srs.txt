Complete English-only version for technical review, development planning, and proposal
preparation.
1. Vision
is a comprehensive open marketplace for Sudan, bringing together buying, selling, renting, services, jobs, real
estate, vehicles, equipment, stores, and businesses in one unified platform.
2. Core Principle
Everything you need, you can find inside MARBOU.
The platform is not specialized only in cars or real estate. It is designed as a comprehensive Marketplace.
3. Competitor Study
Platforms such as OpenSooq and Alsoug may be used to understand the market, user experience, and
marketplace categories, but not to copy their design or product. MARBOU must have its own Sudan-focused
identity and experience, including:
• Dynamic categorization.
• Smart search.
• Listing quality.
• Duplicate and fraud prevention.
• Strong store/business profiles.
• Data Saver.
• Experience optimized for weak internet.
• Advanced tools for sellers and businesses.
• Scalable technical architecture.
